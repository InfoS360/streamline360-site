The Method

The method is called The 4 Installs. Over eight weeks, it installs the Streamline 360 Operating System in four stages:

  1. Operational Risk Map. Every workflow mapped, every bottleneck and owner-dependency found and ranked.
  2. Bottleneck Elimination System. The constraint doing the most damage gets redesigned, with ownership assigned and tracking started.
  3. Ownership and Accountability Layer. Decisions get owners who aren't you, so execution doesn't wait on your desk.
  4. System Validation Under Load. The new system runs against a real week, and what breaks gets fixed before it's called done.
